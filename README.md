# 🦇 Amerith's Curse 🔮

An immersive, gothic expansion pack for Eaglerforge 1.12 that plunges your world into a state of perpetual dread. The sun has set forever, a thick veil of supernatural mist covers the land, and the restless dead have risen to reclaim the surface. Will you become the ultimate hunter, or just another missing soul lost in the shadows?

---

## 🌌 Core Mechanics & Environment

* 🌑 **Perpetual Midnight** — The daylight cycle has been permanently shattered. The kingdom is trapped in an eternal, freezing night.
* 🌫️ **The Blinding Mist** — A dense, custom render-layer fog crawls across the biomes, cutting down your render distance and hiding threats until they are right on top of you.
* 🪓 **The Amerith Veins** — A mysterious, deep-purple radioactive ore buried deep in the bedrock. It strictly requires diamond-tier tools to mine and serves as the catalyst for dark-magic forging.

---

## 👻 The Restless Denizens (Entities & Mobs)

* 👻 **Restless Ghosts** — Fading apparitions that defy gravity. They use custom flight AI math to hover above the ground, periodically phasing entirely out of visibility before executing a chilling proximity attack sound.
* 🧛 **Ancient Vampires** — High-velocity, lethal nighttime predators that burn if they touch light sources, capable of regenerating health upon striking the player.
* 🧟 **The Afflicted (Zombie Re-skin)** — A complete visual overhaul of the standard dead. Their textures are decayed, bloody, and optimized to fit the grim aesthetic of the curse.
* 🎃 **Pumpkin Golems** — Towering, defensive monsters stitched together from rotted vines and carved jack-o'-lanterns. They patrol the fog, hunting down anything that breathes.

---

## 🏰 World Generation

* 🏚️ **The Haunted Mansions** — Sprawling, procedurally generated gothic fortresses hidden deep in the dark oak woods. Packed with traps, hidden loot chests, and dense clusters of monster spawners.

---

## ⚔️ The Monster Slayer's Arsenal (Weapons & Items)

* 🗡️ **The Monster Slayer** — A heavy, dark-forged blade that inflicts massive bonus damage variables specifically against undead, vampire, and ghostly entity targets.
* 🌀 **The Ghost Catcher** — A highly specialized tactical tool. Striking a Restless Ghost triggers a localized smoke particle explosion, safely containing the spirit and dissolving its entity data.
* 🔫 **The Rapid-Fire Uzi** — A ranged weapon that allows you to fight back against the horrors from a distance, firing high-velocity precision projectiles through the fog.
* 🧅 **Wild Onions** — A brand new agricultural crop found growing in swamp biomes. Eat them for a quick nutritional boost, or use them in crafting to repel certain nocturnal predators.
* 🌾 **Cursed Grass (Grass Re-skin)** — The green foliage of the overworld has died out, replaced by a rotted, ash-grey texture profile that blankets the landscape.
